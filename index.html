Extending to c. 0.69 acres, this site at Crocknahattina offers an opportunity to create a home in an attractive countryside setting just c. 4.6km west of Bailieborough, subject to the relevant planning permission being obtained.

The site is currently overgrown and requires clearing, but its position offers real potential. Opening up the ground could reveal impressive views across the surrounding Cavan countryside and create an attractive setting for a future home.

Bailieborough is only a short drive away, providing easy access to shops, schools, sports facilities and everyday amenities, while the site itself enjoys a rural location surrounded by open countryside.

For anyone looking for a site they can shape and develop to suit their own plans, Crocknahattina offers a strong combination of location, site size and outlook, subject to the necessary planning permission.
